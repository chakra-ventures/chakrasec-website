Mobile Security Research & Development

Protection that was once reserved
for the privileged few

We research and build defences against the most advanced Android malware — making military-grade mobile security accessible to everyone who needs it, not just those who can afford it.

What we do

What we believe

The common public deserves to be protected by military-grade cyber security defences — the kind long reserved for Fortune 500 companies and their resources. That inequity is the problem we exist to correct.

What we do

Research-led, mission-driven

Our work spans the full spectrum — from deep technical research into Android malware to direct, hands-on engagement with individuals and organisations facing real threats.

01

Research & Development

Deep technical investigation into advanced Android malware — static analysis, behavioural detection, obfuscation unpacking, and novel detection methods.

02

Targeted Product Development

Building detection and response tooling designed for high-risk individuals and the organisations that support them — not enterprise IT departments.

03

Professional Services

Direct assistance to people and organisations targeted by sophisticated cyber threats — incident analysis, device forensics, and response guidance.

04

Consulting & Proactive Defence

Engagements for organisations seeking to build resilient, proactive defences before threats materialise — not reactive scrambles after the fact.
